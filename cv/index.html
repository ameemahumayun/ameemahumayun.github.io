Interactive Media Lab, LUMS — Research Project Lead
May 2025 – June 2026 · LLM performance on conservative, indirect health language (CHI '26) · Advisor: Dr. Maryam Mustafa · Lahore, Pakistan
  • Led mixed-methods research on culturally situated LLM failures in maternal and sexual/reproductive health communication in Pakistan; accepted to CHI 2026 (CORE A*) and presented in Barcelona. Project associated with Awaaz-e-Sehat and supported by the Gates Foundation.
  • Designed and conducted qualitative fieldwork comprising 40 clinician–patient observations, 22 semi-structured interviews, and 5 focus groups with patients and healthcare practitioners.
  • Adapted the study methodology in response to early field findings, shifting from primarily elicited accounts toward naturalistic observation and clinician interviews to better capture embedded euphemistic and indirect language.
  • Developed through bottom-up qualitative analysis: (1) a novel Domain–Approach framework for classifying indirect health communication by referential domain and communicative strategy; and (2) a decision-tree classification procedure applied across ~95 patient-derived expressions.
  • Co-designed and executed a systematic evaluation of 5 LLMs on 71 culturally grounded Roman Urdu utterances across controlled prompting conditions, with gynecologist validation and statistical model comparison; demonstrated how strong aggregate scores can mask clinically and contextually important failures, and characterized recurrent failure modes including polysemy blindness, script instability, and persona inconsistency.
  • Synthesized qualitative and model-evaluation findings into design recommendations for culturally situated health AI, including terminology management, miscommunication-aware interaction design, and privacy-conscious multimodal input.
