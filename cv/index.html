Research Interests

My research interests center on culturally situated human–AI interaction, examining how social relationships, cultural values, and lived experiences shape people’s experiences with technology. Building on my work in relational privacy, culturally mediated communication, and AI safety in healthcare, I am increasingly interested in how people make sense of AI and negotiate its role in their lives, particularly its psychological, relational, and spiritual dimensions. Through qualitative research and human-centered AI evaluation, I aim to translate these insights into more contextually responsive technology design.

Indirect SRH Language and LLMs in Conservative Contexts — Research Project Lead
May 2025 – April 2026 · Advisor: Dr. Maryam Mustafa · Lahore, Pakistan

  • Conducted 40 clinician–patient observations, 22 semi-structured interviews, and 5 focus groups; adapted fieldwork methods to capture naturalistic indirect communication.
  • Developed a novel Domain–Approach framework classifying indirect SRH language by referential domain and communicative strategy, alongside a decision-tree procedure for categorizing ~95 patient-derived expressions.
  • Co-designed and conducted a systematic evaluation of 5 LLMs on 71 culturally grounded Roman Urdu utterances across controlled prompting conditions, incorporating gynecologist validation and statistical model comparisons; identified clinically consequential failures obscured by aggregate metrics.
  • Synthesized empirical and model-evaluation findings into design recommendations for culturally situated health AI, addressing terminology management, miscommunication-aware interaction design, and privacy-conscious multimodal input.
